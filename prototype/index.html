Can I order a physical print now?

No. The current site is a design preview. Physical ordering, final specifications, prices and delivery options are not yet confirmed.

What kind of photo should I choose?

Start with a sharp, well-lit photo where your pet’s face and markings are easy to see. You can browse styles first, before selecting a photo.

Do these images show the final print texture?

The images here are design examples, not proof of the final physical finish. Texture and depth should be assessed using an identified physical print sample.
